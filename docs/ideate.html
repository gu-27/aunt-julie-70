Concept 1

Memory Mosaic

"Every tile is someone who loves you."

Seventy numbered gold tiles arranged in a grid — one for each memory. Tap any tile to reveal the name and message behind it. The full grid is a striking visual on screen, and could be printed as a keepsake poster for her wall. Simple. Bold. 70 at a glance.

Concept 2

The Memory Jar

"Reach in and pull out a memory."

A digital mason jar overflowing with 70 colorful paper slips — one for each memory. Click any slip to unfurl it and read. Navigate forward and back to keep reading. Whimsical, warm, and completely replayable. Could also be made as a real jar with printed slips for a physical gift.

Concept 3

70 Reasons We Love You

"A numbered list she can read again and again."

Clean, typographic, and elegant. Every memory is a numbered entry — name first, then the message. Scrolling through all 70 feels like turning pages in a book written just for her. Works beautifully as a digital page she can bookmark, or printed as a bound booklet.

Concept 4

A Life in Chapters

"Seven decades. Seventy memories. One remarkable life."

Memories organized by the chapters of Julie's life — from early years through today. Each decade expands to reveal the memories that belong to it. Narrative and meaningful; helps her see her life as a whole story rather than a list of moments. Beautiful as an interactive page or a printed timeline display.

Concept 5

Polaroid Wall

"A wall of memories, each one hand-delivered."

Every memory is a polaroid card pinned to a warm cork wall — slightly tilted, full of personality. Hover to straighten and read. Works digitally as a keepsake page, or each card can be printed on cardstock and displayed on a real corkboard or strung with lights. Very tactile, very her.
